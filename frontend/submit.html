Spotted a camera? Drop a pin, add a photo if you can, and submit. Reports are reviewed by a person before they appear on the map. Nothing you enter about yourself is published. Prefer email? Write to retina@perdidoinstitute.com.

Project NOLA cameras are usually larger than typical business cameras, often on a pan-tilt mount, sometimes with a Project NOLA sticker or sign on the host business. A photo of the camera plus one of the surroundings helps reviewers confirm it.
