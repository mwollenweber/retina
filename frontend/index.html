About this map

Retina is a project of the Perdido Institute: a self-hosted, community-collected map of surveillance cameras in New Orleans. The focus is Project NOLA, the private camera network that has fed live facial recognition alerts to police.

Sources: field reports from the public (each reviewed with photos before it appears), the City of New Orleans traffic camera dataset on data.nola.gov, the City's public safety (RTCC) camera map on nolagis.maps.arcgis.com, and OpenStreetMap.

Disclaimer

Camera locations come from public records or are best-effort placements, and may be out of date or wrong. Most Project NOLA points are suspected cameras: a camera was seen at or near the address, but its network and operator are not confirmed unless a popup says so. Nothing here is an official record, and it should not be relied on for any legal, safety, or investigative purpose.

Use the Street View link in a popup to check a location yourself. Found a mistake? Use Report a camera and describe the correction.

Questions, corrections, or records to share: retina@perdidoinstitute.com.
